# Rituals assets

## Moon surface

`img/moon-color.jpg` and `img/moon-height.jpg` are from NASA's Scientific
Visualization Studio, [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/).
Color: LROC WAC mosaic. Elevation: LRO / LOLA. Visualization: Ernie Wright.

Downloaded source files:

## Marble hands

`img/ritual-hands.png` was generated with the built-in Imagegen tool for this
project. The transparent alpha channel is preserved. The moon is rendered
separately with Three.js; it is not part of the generated image.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG foreground cutout for a dark mystical tarot website, 1024x1024 square. Create two elegant ancient Greek marble statue hands and forearms, realistic weathered grey stone, delicate anatomical fingers, subtle warm grey patina and fine cracks. Both forearms rise from the bottom edge, left hand at left, right hand at right, palms face upward and inward, gently cupping an INVISIBLE LARGE SPHERE in the empty center. Composition: imaginary sphere center at x50% y39%, diameter 48% of canvas. Fingers curve naturally along the LOWER LEFT and LOWER RIGHT edges of that empty sphere, approximately x25% and x75% y43%-65%; wrists at x33% and x67% y76%; forearms reach bottom edge. Leave the entire center above y58% mostly empty for a separately rendered 3D moon. Sophisticated photographic sculpture cutout, directional soft light from upper left, deep grey sculptural shadows. Genuinely transparent alpha background everywhere except hands and forearms, including gap between hands. No sphere, no moon, no plants, no backdrop, no text, no symbols, no glow, no border. Isolated statue hands only.
