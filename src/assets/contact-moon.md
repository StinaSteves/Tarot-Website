# Contact moon

`img/contact-moon-color.jpg` and `img/contact-moon-height.jpg` use NASA's
[CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), visualization by Ernie Wright.
Color: LROC WAC mosaic. Elevation: LRO / LOLA.

Sources:
- https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
- https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/ldem_3_8bit.jpg

Rendered as a rotating Three.js sphere above the contact illustration, with
a clipped foreground fingertip. The original illustration remains the fallback
when WebGL is unavailable.
